GleanGrid Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

GleanGrid helps users review and organize photos and videos stored in their iPhone Photos library.

Data Collection

GleanGrid does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

Photos and Videos

GleanGrid accesses your Photos library only after you grant permission.

Photo and video analysis is performed on your device. Your photos and videos are not uploaded to GleanGrid servers.

Deletion

GleanGrid does not automatically delete photos or videos. Items are deleted only after you review them and explicitly confirm the deletion.

Analytics and Advertising

GleanGrid does not use advertising, tracking, analytics, or third-party data collection SDKs.

In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. GleanGrid does not receive your payment card information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: oz.cp@hotmail.com
