Common Topics

GleanGrid analyzes photos and videos directly on your iPhone.

Your photos and videos are not uploaded to GleanGrid servers.

GleanGrid only deletes items after you review and explicitly confirm the deletion.

Deleted items are moved to the Recently Deleted album in Photos according to Apple's Photos behavior.
